# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Vessel-investment analysts and owners who assess a vessel acquisition. Confirmed in the product-context question answered on 18 October 2026.

## Product Purpose

Help users turn explicit vessel, operating, financing and exit assumptions into understandable cash flows and returns. Users must be able to inspect why a number changes and reproduce their study.

## Positioning

A single-vessel maritime acquisition calculator with one transparent financial engine, timed costs, debt settlement and consistent investor reporting. No claim of proprietary market data or investment assurance.

## Operating Context

Use a desktop browser for detailed analysis and a phone for review or input changes. Work from broker information and analyst assumptions. Save and move studies through validated JSON files. Publish through GitHub; support local use.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The user confirmed a device-first calculator with import/export and no account required. Studies remain on the device unless the user exports and shares a file. Do not use the legacy Supabase connection or collect credentials. No simulated AI. Core calculations and exported results must match. The model uses annual periods and USD; limitations must be explicit. Exact equations belong in the engine contract and manual.

## Brand Commitments

CapeAI is the current repository name. The user requests a complete UI/UX redesign suitable for a professional operational tool. The old black/gold theme is evidence, not a binding visual direction. Use plain English and precise financial terms.

## Evidence on Hand

The forensic snapshot and report are in forensic-review-2026-10-18. The approved implementation scope is IMPLEMENTATION-BRIEF.md. Synthetic example inputs must be labelled as examples. No customer, performance, regulatory, or market-data claims are supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make every important result explainable.
- Validate assumptions before presenting conclusions.
- Make data ownership clear and work recoverable.
- Keep one financial truth across views and reports.
- Use professional clarity and task completion as the design standard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive phone and desktop web, keyboard access, clear focus, semantic labels, readable financial tables, touch-friendly controls and sufficient contrast. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA practices and verify the main journey.
