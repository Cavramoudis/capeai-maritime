# CapeAI glossary

Use these definitions when reading the application and manual. Some financial terms have different definitions in other products or loan agreements.

The [model manual](MANUAL.md) gives the exact equations and calculation boundaries.

| Term | Meaning in CapeAI |
|---|---|
| Acquisition | The purchase of the vessel and the associated initial investment. |
| Acquisition costs | Transaction costs entered separately from the vessel purchase price. |
| Additional equity | New investor cash required when an annual investor cash flow is negative. |
| Annual period | One model year. Operating cash flows occur at its end for return calculations. |
| Asset cash flow | Cash generated by the vessel investment before debt financing and investor carry. It includes operating fees and unlevered tax assumptions. |
| Asset discount rate | The required annual return used to discount asset cash flows. |
| Asset IRR | The internal rate of return calculated from the asset cash-flow stream. |
| Asset NPV | The net present value calculated from asset cash flows and the asset discount rate. |
| Assumption | A user-supplied estimate or contractual input. The application does not verify its commercial accuracy. |
| Balloon | The initial debt fraction left outside scheduled equal-principal amortization. It becomes payable at maturity or earlier sale. |
| Base case | The study's unchanged assumptions used as the comparison reference. |
| Basis | The specific cash-flow stream, cost definition, or amount used in a calculation. |
| Book value | The remaining modeled tax basis after tax depreciation and CAPEX additions. It is not a vessel market valuation. |
| Breakeven charter rate | The daily hire needed to cover the costs included in the specified breakeven measure. |
| Brokerage | A commercial fee calculated as a percentage of gross charter revenue. |
| Browser storage | Data stored by the browser for the application's origin. Clearing this storage removes local studies. |
| CAPEX | Capital expenditure. CapeAI deducts the entered payment in its specified year. |
| Carry | The sponsor's stated percentage of positive aggregate nominal equity profit, charged at exit. |
| Cash deficit | A negative cash amount under the stated basis. Annual investor deficits require additional equity in this model. |
| Cash flow | A cash inflow or cash outflow at a specified model time. |
| CFADS | Cash flow available for debt service under CapeAI's stated operating basis. A lender may use another definition. |
| Charter | An agreement for the use of a vessel. The model uses a supplied daily hire assumption. |
| Charter rate | Hire earned per earning day, in USD/day. |
| Closing debt | Debt remaining after the year's scheduled principal, maturity repayment, and sale settlement. |
| Contribution | Cash supplied by equity investors. It is negative in the investor cash-flow stream. |
| Cost days | Days used to accrue operating costs in each model year. |
| Debt | Borrowed acquisition funding. CapeAI models one fixed-rate loan. |
| Debt arrangement fee | A time-zero charge calculated on the initial debt draw. |
| Debt draw | The loan cash received at acquisition. |
| Debt service | Interest plus the principal repayment included in the stated service measure. |
| Declining-value exit | A sale assumption calculated by reducing purchase price at a constant annual percentage. |
| Depreciation | A reduction of a modeled asset basis. Exit-value decline and tax depreciation are separate assumptions. |
| Discount factor | The multiplier that converts a future cash flow to time-zero value. |
| Discount rate | The required annual return for the cash-flow basis being valued. |
| Disposal cost | A selling cost calculated as a percentage of gross terminal vessel value. |
| Distribution | Positive investor cash received during the model. |
| DWT | Deadweight tonnage. CapeAI stores this descriptive vessel field but does not use it in financial calculations. |
| Downside case | A comparison with less favorable selected assumptions. It is not a probability forecast. |
| DSCR | Debt-service coverage ratio. It compares operating cash available with the defined debt-service amount. |
| Earning days | Days that generate charter revenue in a model year. |
| Equity | Investor funding after debt has funded its specified part of the purchase. |
| Equity cash flow | Cash paid by or returned to investors after the model's financing, fees, tax, and carry. |
| Equity discount rate | The required annual investor return used to discount equity cash flows. |
| Equity IRR | The internal rate of return calculated from the final investor cash-flow stream. |
| Equity multiple | Total positive investor distributions divided by all investor contributions. It does not measure timing. |
| Equity NPV | The net present value calculated from final investor cash flows and the equity discount rate. |
| Exit | Disposal of the vessel at the end of the holding period. |
| Exit proceeds | Vessel sale cash after the specific deductions shown. Gross and net proceeds must remain distinct. |
| Fee | A charge with a stated percentage or cash basis. A fee is not automatically included in another cost input. |
| Fixed interest rate | An annual borrowing rate that remains unchanged throughout the loan. |
| Gross revenue | Charter rate multiplied by earning days, before brokerage and other costs. |
| Holding period | The number of complete model years from acquisition to vessel disposal. |
| Hurdle rate | A required return used to judge an investment. CapeAI's simple carry has no hurdle waterfall. |
| Inflation | The annual percentage change applied to operating costs. |
| Initial equity | Investor cash required at acquisition after the debt draw. |
| Interest | A financing expense calculated from opening debt and the annual loan rate. |
| IRR | Internal rate of return. The annual discount rate that makes the specified cash-flow stream's NPV zero. |
| JSON | A structured text format used to export and import a study. It is not encryption. |
| LDT | Light displacement tonnage. It is the physical quantity used for the entered scrap-value calculation. |
| Legacy study | A study created by an earlier application with another data or calculation structure. |
| Leverage | The use of debt to fund part of the acquisition. It changes investor cash flows and risk. |
| Liquidity | The ability to meet cash payments when due. Positive lifetime profit does not establish annual liquidity. |
| Local draft | The current editable study retained by the browser, when storage is available. |
| LTV | Loan-to-value percentage. In CapeAI, it means initial debt divided by vessel purchase price. |
| Management fee | An annual charge calculated on original vessel purchase price in this model. |
| Manual exit | A terminal vessel value entered directly by the user. |
| Maturity | The model year when all remaining contractual debt becomes due. |
| Model version | An identifier for the calculation rules used by a release. |
| MOIC | Multiple on invested capital. CapeAI uses its defined equity multiple. |
| Named study | A saved entry in the browser's study library. It is separate from the editable draft. |
| Net cash flow | Inflows less outflows for the stated period and basis. |
| Nominal amount | A cash amount stated in the currency units expected for its payment period. |
| Nominal profit | Undiscounted distributions less contributions. It does not adjust for the time value of money. |
| NPV | Net present value. The discounted sum of all cash flows, including the initial investment. |
| Off-hire | Time without charter revenue. Lower earning days can represent its effect. |
| Opening debt | The debt balance before the current year's interest and principal payments. |
| Operating funding breakeven | The daily charter rate required to cover the defined annual operating and contractual financing cash requirements. |
| Operating surplus | Gross charter revenue less OPEX, brokerage, and the annual management fee. |
| OPEX | Operating expenditure, entered as a daily cost and accrued over cost days. |
| Outstanding debt | The loan principal still owed at a specified time. |
| Principal | The borrowed amount, excluding interest and financing fees. |
| Required return | The return an investor or capital provider requires for the assumed risk. |
| Residual value | The vessel's assumed value at disposal. CapeAI uses the term terminal value for this amount. |
| Return basis | The cash flows and fees included in a reported return. |
| ROI | Return on investment. Where used, CapeAI defines it as nominal equity profit divided by total equity contributions. |
| Scenario | A complete recalculation using specified changed assumptions. |
| Scheduled principal | The equal annual principal amount due before any additional balloon or early-sale settlement. |
| Schema version | An identifier for the structure of an exported study. |
| Scrap price | The assumed USD value per light displacement tonne at disposal. |
| Scrap value | Light displacement tonnage multiplied by the entered scrap price. |
| Sensitivity | The change in an output when selected assumptions change. |
| Sign change | A switch between negative and positive nonzero cash flows. Several switches can make IRR interpretation ambiguous. |
| SPV | Special purpose vehicle. A legal entity used to hold an investment; CapeAI does not create one. |
| Study | One named set of vessel-investment assumptions and its calculated results. |
| Survey | A scheduled inspection or related maintenance event. The analyst supplies its cash cost and payment year. |
| Tax basis | The modeled amount available for future tax depreciation and sale-gain calculations. |
| Tax depreciation | A noncash deduction calculated from the opening modeled tax basis. |
| Tax rate | The user-entered percentage used by CapeAI's simplified tax formula. |
| Tenor | The loan's contractual duration in complete model years. |
| Terminal value | Gross vessel value at the end of the holding period, before selling costs and debt repayment. |
| Time zero | The acquisition date in the annual model, before the first operating year. |
| Unlevered | Calculated before debt funding, debt service, and debt-related tax benefits. |
| Upside case | A comparison with more favorable selected assumptions. It is not a probability forecast. |
| Validation | Checks that inputs satisfy the supported types and model limits. It does not verify market plausibility. |
| Working capital | The entered initial operating funding amount, held constant and released fully at exit. |
| Year zero | Another name for time zero. |

## Unit and notation rules

| Notation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `USD` or `$` | United States dollars. |
| `USD/day` | Dollars per day. |
| `USD/tonne` | Dollars per metric tonne. |
| `%` | Percentage points entered by the user. Enter `7` for 7%. |
| Decimal fraction | A percentage divided by 100 for use in equations. |
| `×` | Multiplication. |
| `Σ` | Sum over the specified periods or items. |
| `max(a, b)` | The greater of `a` and `b`. |
| `min(a, b)` | The smaller of `a` and `b`. |
| `t` | The current year index. Time zero has index `0`. |
| `H` | The final holding year. |
| Subscript | A variable's period or basis identifier. |

## Terms to keep distinct

**Price and value.** Price is the purchase input. Discounted cash-flow value depends on future cash flows and the required return.

**Profit and liquidity.** A profitable study can require more cash before the vessel is sold.

**Depreciation and CAPEX.** Depreciation affects the simplified tax base. CAPEX is an actual modeled cash payment.

**Asset return and equity return.** Debt changes investor funding and payments. It does not change the vessel's gross charter revenue.

**Scenarios and forecast probabilities.** The tool recalculates entered assumptions without assigning their likelihood.
