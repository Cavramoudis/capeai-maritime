CapeAI glossary

Use these definitions when reading the application and manual. Some financial terms have different definitions in other products or loan agreements.

The model manual gives the exact equations and calculation boundaries.

TermMeaning in CapeAI
AcquisitionThe purchase of the vessel and the associated initial investment.
Acquisition costsTransaction costs entered separately from the vessel purchase price.
Additional equityNew investor cash required when an annual investor cash flow is negative.
Annual periodOne model year. Operating cash flows occur at its end for return calculations.
Asset cash flowCash generated by the vessel investment before debt financing and investor carry. It includes operating fees and unlevered tax assumptions.
Asset discount rateThe required annual return used to discount asset cash flows.
Asset IRRThe internal rate of return calculated from the asset cash-flow stream.
Asset NPVThe net present value calculated from asset cash flows and the asset discount rate.
AssumptionA user-supplied estimate or contractual input. The application does not verify its commercial accuracy.
BalloonThe initial debt fraction left outside scheduled equal-principal amortization. It becomes payable at maturity or earlier sale.
Base caseThe study's unchanged assumptions used as the comparison reference.
BasisThe specific cash-flow stream, cost definition, or amount used in a calculation.
Book valueThe remaining modeled tax basis after tax depreciation and CAPEX additions. It is not a vessel market valuation.
Breakeven charter rateThe daily hire needed to cover the costs included in the specified breakeven measure.
BrokerageA commercial fee calculated as a percentage of gross charter revenue.
Browser storageData stored by the browser for the application's origin. Clearing this storage removes local studies.
CAPEXCapital expenditure. CapeAI deducts the entered payment in its specified year.
CarryThe sponsor's stated percentage of positive aggregate nominal equity profit, charged at exit.
Cash deficitA negative cash amount under the stated basis. Annual investor deficits require additional equity in this model.
Cash flowA cash inflow or cash outflow at a specified model time.
CFADSCash flow available for debt service under CapeAI's stated operating basis. A lender may use another definition.
CharterAn agreement for the use of a vessel. The model uses a supplied daily hire assumption.
Charter rateHire earned per earning day, in USD/day.
Closing debtDebt remaining after the year's scheduled principal, maturity repayment, and sale settlement.
ContributionCash supplied by equity investors. It is negative in the investor cash-flow stream.
Cost daysDays used to accrue operating costs in each model year.
DebtBorrowed acquisition funding. CapeAI models one fixed-rate loan.
Debt arrangement feeA time-zero charge calculated on the initial debt draw.
Debt drawThe loan cash received at acquisition.
Debt serviceInterest plus the principal repayment included in the stated service measure.
Declining-value exitA sale assumption calculated by reducing purchase price at a constant annual percentage.
DepreciationA reduction of a modeled asset basis. Exit-value decline and tax depreciation are separate assumptions.
Discount factorThe multiplier that converts a future cash flow to time-zero value.
Discount rateThe required annual return for the cash-flow basis being valued.
Disposal costA selling cost calculated as a percentage of gross terminal vessel value.
DistributionPositive investor cash received during the model.
DWTDeadweight tonnage. CapeAI stores this descriptive vessel field but does not use it in financial calculations.
Downside caseA comparison with less favorable selected assumptions. It is not a probability forecast.
DSCRDebt-service coverage ratio. It compares operating cash available with the defined debt-service amount.
Earning daysDays that generate charter revenue in a model year.
EquityInvestor funding after debt has funded its specified part of the purchase.
Equity cash flowCash paid by or returned to investors after the model's financing, fees, tax, and carry.
Equity discount rateThe required annual investor return used to discount equity cash flows.
Equity IRRThe internal rate of return calculated from the final investor cash-flow stream.
Equity multipleTotal positive investor distributions divided by all investor contributions. It does not measure timing.
Equity NPVThe net present value calculated from final investor cash flows and the equity discount rate.
ExitDisposal of the vessel at the end of the holding period.
Exit proceedsVessel sale cash after the specific deductions shown. Gross and net proceeds must remain distinct.
FeeA charge with a stated percentage or cash basis. A fee is not automatically included in another cost input.
Fixed interest rateAn annual borrowing rate that remains unchanged throughout the loan.
Gross revenueCharter rate multiplied by earning days, before brokerage and other costs.
Holding periodThe number of complete model years from acquisition to vessel disposal.
Hurdle rateA required return used to judge an investment. CapeAI's simple carry has no hurdle waterfall.
InflationThe annual percentage change applied to operating costs.
Initial equityInvestor cash required at acquisition after the debt draw.
InterestA financing expense calculated from opening debt and the annual loan rate.
IRRInternal rate of return. The annual discount rate that makes the specified cash-flow stream's NPV zero.
JSONA structured text format used to export and import a study. It is not encryption.
LDTLight displacement tonnage. It is the physical quantity used for the entered scrap-value calculation.
Legacy studyA study created by an earlier application with another data or calculation structure.
LeverageThe use of debt to fund part of the acquisition. It changes investor cash flows and risk.
LiquidityThe ability to meet cash payments when due. Positive lifetime profit does not establish annual liquidity.
Local draftThe current editable study retained by the browser, when storage is available.
LTVLoan-to-value percentage. In CapeAI, it means initial debt divided by vessel purchase price.
Management feeAn annual charge calculated on original vessel purchase price in this model.
Manual exitA terminal vessel value entered directly by the user.
MaturityThe model year when all remaining contractual debt becomes due.
Model versionAn identifier for the calculation rules used by a release.
MOICMultiple on invested capital. CapeAI uses its defined equity multiple.
Named studyA saved entry in the browser's study library. It is separate from the editable draft.
Net cash flowInflows less outflows for the stated period and basis.
Nominal amountA cash amount stated in the currency units expected for its payment period.
Nominal profitUndiscounted distributions less contributions. It does not adjust for the time value of money.
NPVNet present value. The discounted sum of all cash flows, including the initial investment.
Off-hireTime without charter revenue. Lower earning days can represent its effect.
Opening debtThe debt balance before the current year's interest and principal payments.
Operating funding breakevenThe daily charter rate required to cover the defined annual operating and contractual financing cash requirements.
Operating surplusGross charter revenue less OPEX, brokerage, and the annual management fee.
OPEXOperating expenditure, entered as a daily cost and accrued over cost days.
Outstanding debtThe loan principal still owed at a specified time.
PrincipalThe borrowed amount, excluding interest and financing fees.
Required returnThe return an investor or capital provider requires for the assumed risk.
Residual valueThe vessel's assumed value at disposal. CapeAI uses the term terminal value for this amount.
Return basisThe cash flows and fees included in a reported return.
ROIReturn on investment. Where used, CapeAI defines it as nominal equity profit divided by total equity contributions.
ScenarioA complete recalculation using specified changed assumptions.
Scheduled principalThe equal annual principal amount due before any additional balloon or early-sale settlement.
Schema versionAn identifier for the structure of an exported study.
Scrap priceThe assumed USD value per light displacement tonne at disposal.
Scrap valueLight displacement tonnage multiplied by the entered scrap price.
SensitivityThe change in an output when selected assumptions change.
Sign changeA switch between negative and positive nonzero cash flows. Several switches can make IRR interpretation ambiguous.
SPVSpecial purpose vehicle. A legal entity used to hold an investment; CapeAI does not create one.
StudyOne named set of vessel-investment assumptions and its calculated results.
SurveyA scheduled inspection or related maintenance event. The analyst supplies its cash cost and payment year.
Tax basisThe modeled amount available for future tax depreciation and sale-gain calculations.
Tax depreciationA noncash deduction calculated from the opening modeled tax basis.
Tax rateThe user-entered percentage used by CapeAI's simplified tax formula.
TenorThe loan's contractual duration in complete model years.
Terminal valueGross vessel value at the end of the holding period, before selling costs and debt repayment.
Time zeroThe acquisition date in the annual model, before the first operating year.
UnleveredCalculated before debt funding, debt service, and debt-related tax benefits.
Upside caseA comparison with more favorable selected assumptions. It is not a probability forecast.
ValidationChecks that inputs satisfy the supported types and model limits. It does not verify market plausibility.
Working capitalThe entered initial operating funding amount, held constant and released fully at exit.
Year zeroAnother name for time zero.

Unit and notation rules

NotationMeaning
USD or $United States dollars.
USD/dayDollars per day.
USD/tonneDollars per metric tonne.
%Percentage points entered by the user. Enter 7 for 7%.
Decimal fractionA percentage divided by 100 for use in equations.
×Multiplication.
ΣSum over the specified periods or items.
max(a, b)The greater of a and b.
min(a, b)The smaller of a and b.
tThe current year index. Time zero has index 0.
HThe final holding year.
SubscriptA variable's period or basis identifier.

Terms to keep distinct

Price and value. Price is the purchase input. Discounted cash-flow value depends on future cash flows and the required return.

Profit and liquidity. A profitable study can require more cash before the vessel is sold.

Depreciation and CAPEX. Depreciation affects the simplified tax base. CAPEX is an actual modeled cash payment.

Asset return and equity return. Debt changes investor funding and payments. It does not change the vessel's gross charter revenue.

Scenarios and forecast probabilities. The tool recalculates entered assumptions without assigning their likelihood.
